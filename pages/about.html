    About the Club

    A local darts platform built around real club workflows

    Famagusta Dart Club uses this website as the public face of the club and as the internal operations layer for registrations, brackets, membership reviews, and community updates.

    View tournaments Join the club

    What the club is doing here

    The public site is meant to answer the questions that usually get scattered across chats and social media: which tournaments are open, who is playing, what the bracket looks like, what was published recently, and how someone joins the club.

    What the platform now covers

    The current release includes membership applications, player profiles, tournament registration, public bracket viewing, admin tournament operations, blog publishing, and club media management.

    Core workflows

    Tournaments

    Players and guests can follow open registrations, public fixtures, bracket progress, and completed results without needing the admin console.

    Membership

    Membership status is kept separate from login role, so the club can review real membership paperwork without confusing it with site permissions.

    Public player pages

    Player profiles now expose safe public statistics, placement history, recent results, and links back into the tournament flow.

    Admin operations

    Managers and admins can create tournaments, review membership requests, manage content, and work inside the consolidated tournament detail view.

    Contact and next action

    For direct club contact, use the official Facebook page or phone line listed on the homepage. For membership onboarding, use the Join the Club page so the application lands in the review queue instead of getting lost in chat.
